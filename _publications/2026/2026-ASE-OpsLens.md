abstract: >-
    Root cause analysis (RCA) for cloud services requires correlating multi-modal observability data, yet current LLM-based approaches over-rely on system-specific artifacts such as runbooks and struggle to ensure the quality and reuse of LLM-generated diagnostic tools. We propose OpsLens, an RCA framework that combines modality-specialized multi-agent collaboration with quality-focused tool management, including feedback-driven refinement and a tool graph with scoreboard. On OpenRCA, OpsLens achieves a score rate of 0.80, outperforming baselines by 1.79–4.75×; tested on a Huawei platform since June 2025, it achieves over 80% RCA accuracy and reduces diagnosis time from over an hour to under five minutes.
