abstract: >-
    Modern cloud services rely on a high volume of changes to drive rapid innovation, yet these changes are a primary cause of production incidents. Through a study of ByteDance's cloud platform, we find that although high-risk changes undergo extensive scrutiny, 78.1% of change-induced incidents originate from the large volume of low-risk changes that receive minimal review, making exhaustive manual review infeasible at scale. To address this, we present Aegis, a knowledge-driven system that provides interpretable change risk assessment by automatically constructing a knowledge base from historical operational data, distilling it into generalized risk heuristics, and identifying relevant precedent cases.
