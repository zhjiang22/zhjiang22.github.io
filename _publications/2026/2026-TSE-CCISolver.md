abstract: >-
    Comments within code are a crucial foundation for software documentation, yet code-comment inconsistency (CCI) can undermine software development, testing, and maintenance. Analyzing existing datasets reveals that a substantial portion of samples are mislabeled, motivating CCIBench, a refined high-quality dataset for training and evaluating method-level CCI approaches. Building on it, we present CCISolver, an end-to-end LLM-based framework that both detects and repairs inconsistencies, achieving a new state-of-the-art detection F1-score of 89.54% while running faster than prior baselines.
