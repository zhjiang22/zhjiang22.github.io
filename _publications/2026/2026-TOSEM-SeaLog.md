abstract: >-
    System logs are critical for software reliability, yet log-based anomaly detection methods often falter in large-scale cloud systems due to high resource consumption and poor adaptability to evolving logs. We present SeaLog, an accurate, lightweight, and adaptive framework that combines the efficiency of traditional data-driven methods with the zero/few-shot learning ability of LLMs: a lightweight detection agent built on an n-gram probabilistic tree, and an LLM backbone analyzer enhanced by in-context learning, both continuously improved by anomalies confirmed by on-site engineers. SeaLog achieves F1 scores of 0.949–0.999 while running 2× to 5× faster with 5% to 41% of the memory, and has been deployed in Huawei Cloud.
